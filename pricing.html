How 3-Way Match Billing Protects Your Corporate Budget

Traditional vendor billing forces finance teams to audit thousands of manual paper slips at the end of each month. AdhocX replaces this chaos with automated, real-time programmatic verification.

  • Roster Demand Match: Every billed leg must trace back to an authorized employee shift record published by HR.
  • PostGIS Telemetry Validation: Actual distance is computed from geofenced pickup and drop events, stripping phantom detours.
  • Contract Rate Card Lock: System applies your agreed MSA rate cards automatically. No ad-hoc markup by dispatchers.
