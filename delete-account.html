Account & Personal Data Deletion Request

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

Google Play Developer Policy Notice: This web portal allows registered Driver Partners and Enterprise Users of the AdhocX Driver: Corporate Fleet mobile application (Package: com.adhocx.app) to submit a formal account and personal data deletion request without needing the app installed.

Method 1: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

If you still have the AdhocX application installed on your Android device:

  • Open the AdhocX Driver app.
  • Tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  • Tap Account Settings → Request Account Deletion.
  • Confirm your registered phone number via OTP to trigger immediate account deactivation.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (Email / Form)

If you have uninstalled the app or are unable to log in, you can request account deletion directly from this page:

Please ensure you email us from or mention your registered phone number and full legal name so our security team can verify ownership of the account.
🗑 Submit Deletion Request via Email (support@adhocx.com)

What Data is Permanently Deleted?

Upon verification of your deletion request, the following records are permanently removed from our active production databases within 30 calendar days:

  • Profile & Contact Details: Full name, email address, residential address, emergency contacts, profile photo / selfie.
  • Uploaded Commercial Documents: Digital copies of Commercial Driving License, Vehicle RC (Registration Certificate), Commercial Insurance, and Vehicle Fitness documents.
  • Telemetry & Location History: Historical GPS trace logs, duty navigation routes, and device identifiers.
  • Authentication Credentials: Password hashes, session tokens, and push notification tokens.

What Data is Retained for Legal Compliance?

In accordance with the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, the Indian Income Tax Act, 1961, and statutory audit guidelines, certain records cannot be immediately erased:

  • Completed Financial & Payout Records: Records of settled bank payouts, TDS deductions, and GST invoices are retained for the statutory 7-year audit requirement.
  • Safety Incident Logs: Any ongoing police/traffic incident investigations or unresolved passenger safety reports must be maintained until formal legal closure.
