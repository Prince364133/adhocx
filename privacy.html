Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Estemporaneo Technologies Private Limited ("AdhocX", "we", "our", or "us") operates the B2B commercial transport marketplace platform connecting corporate enterprise clients ("Vendors" or "Clients") with verified commercial transport service providers and chauffeurs ("Drivers"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information across our web applications, APIs, and mobile apps.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your relationship with AdhocX (Corporate Client, Passenger, or Driver), we collect:

  • Corporate Account Data: Company name, GSTIN, registered address, billing contacts, designated travel managers, and authorized bookers.
  • Driver KYC Data: Commercial Driving License, Yellow Board Registration Certificate (RC), commercial vehicle insurance, vehicle fitness certificate, Aadhaar number, PAN, and bank account / UPI details for payouts.
  • Location & Telemetry Data: Precise GPS location of drivers during active duties, route telemetry, trip start/end timestamps, and boarding OTP verifications.
  • Communication Data: In-app messaging, automated trip dispatch SMS, emergency helpline logs, and customer support audio recordings.

3. How We Use Collected Data

We process data strictly for operational, safety, and regulatory fulfillment:

  • Automating reverse-auction dispatching and vehicle allocation within 15-minute SLA windows.
  • Facilitating 1-hour automated bank refunds for canceled or unfulfilled bookings.
  • Calculating and disbursing driver earnings, weekly payouts, and ₹2,000 Century Milestone bonuses.
  • Providing live GPS tracking links to corporate control rooms and designated employee safety emergency desks.
  • Complying with statutory Motor Vehicles Act (MVA) reporting requirements and tax accounting.

4. Data Protection & Security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3 protocol. Sensitive financial data, bank account numbers, and personal identifiers are stored with AES-256 encryption at rest. Access to operational databases is restricted to authorized operations personnel under strict multi-factor authentication and role-based access control (RBAC).

5. Location Data & Background Telemetry (Prominent Disclosure)

AdhocX Driver collects and processes real-time GPS location data, including background location data (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION):

  • Purpose of Background Location: To enable real-time fleet allocation, live passenger dispatching, and safety tracking for corporate client control rooms while scheduled employee commute duties are in progress, even when the app is closed or minimized.
  • Collection Window: Location data is strictly collected ONLY while the Driver has explicitly toggled their status to "On-Duty".
  • Termination: Location collection terminates immediately once the Driver toggles "Off-Duty", completes all assigned duties, or logs out of the app. Location data is never sold or used for targeted third-party advertising.

6. User Account & Personal Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play User Data policies and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, users and Drivers have the absolute right to request deletion of their account and associated personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account directly inside the AdhocX Mobile App by navigating to Profile > Settings > Request Account Deletion.
  • Web Deletion Portal: You can request account deletion online without installing the app by visiting https://www.adhocx.com/delete-account or emailing our Data Protection Officer at support@adhocx.com with the subject "Account & Data Deletion Request" from your registered phone/email.
  • Data Deletion Scope: Upon verification, your profile data, contact information, uploaded KYC identification documents (DL, RC, Insurance photos), and telemetry logs are permanently purged from active systems within thirty (30) days.
  • Statutory Retention Exception: Completed financial payout histories and GST invoicing records are retained strictly for the statutory audit period required under the Indian Income Tax Act and Companies Act.
