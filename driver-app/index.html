Partner Verification Standard

Strictly Commercial Yellow-Plate Vehicles

To maintain enterprise safety contracts, AdhocX only onboards commercially registered, insured, and permit-holding passenger vehicles.

Eligible 4-Seater Sedans:

Maruti Dzire, Toyota Etios, Hyundai Aura, Tata Tigor EV (Yellow-Plate T/TR registration).

Eligible 6/7-Seater MUVs & SUVs:

Maruti Ertiga, XL6, Kia Carens, Toyota Innova Crysta (Commercial yellow board).

Direct Contract Model:

You contract directly with AdhocX. Payouts are made straight to your verified bank account without intermediary cuts.

Driver Partner Technology

Everything You Need to Manage Your Shifts

Built for operational ease, quick boarding verification, and zero payment disputes.

Paperless Onboarding & Verification

Upload your commercial driving license, vehicle RC, RTA fitness, insurance, and bank account details directly through the app. Digital review and activation within 24 business hours.

Pre-Scheduled Route Offers

Receive advance shift offers showing exact pickup zone, scheduled pickup time, stop count, total route distance, and agreed contracted payout before you accept.

Ordered Stops & Boarding Scanner

Integrated turn-by-turn navigation guides you to each employee's pickup waypoint. Verify passenger boarding instantly using dynamic 4-digit OTP or QR code.

Transparent Payouts to Bank

Earnings are generated automatically upon trip completion. Review submitted, approved, and paid payouts with bank confirmation references in your in-app ledger.

24/7 Dispatch Desk Assistance

Direct in-app contact with our operations desk for vehicle breakdowns, road blockages, passenger no-shows, or route emergencies.

Compliance Alerts & Safety Training

Proactive alerts before insurance, fitness, or driving licenses expire. Access mandatory corporate passenger etiquette and safety guidelines directly in the app.
