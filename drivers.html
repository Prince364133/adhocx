Join AdhocX as a commercial driver partner in Telangana or Hyderabad, complete your onboarding documentation, and start fulfilling corporate rides. The ₹2,000 bonus is credited directly into your verified bank account upon meeting the 100-ride milestone. Terms and conditions apply.

Which vehicles are eligible?

In addition to our regular scheduled Monday settlements, driver partners can request on-demand cashouts anytime via the app. Funds are transferred via IMPS/UPI rails directly into your bank account within 1 hour.
