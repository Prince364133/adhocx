# Employee App User Guide

**Evidence status:** Verified against routes and employee repositories.

## Sign In

Open the app and use password or OTP login. Company email verification may
send an OTP to complete login. Your account must belong to an eligible
corporate tenant and employee record.

## Your Schedule

The app shows:

- Today/upcoming trip
- Daily, weekly and monthly schedule
- Shift details
- Driver and vehicle details
- Cancellation/change request states

## Before Pickup

Review:

- Pickup location
- Pickup time
- Driver
- Vehicle
- Live cab position
- Network/GPS state

If GPS or network information is unavailable, use Retry and await a fresh
update. Do not treat stale location as a live position.

## Boarding

When instructed by the app/driver:

1. Open the boarding verification view.
2. Present the OTP or QR code.
3. Wait for successful boarding confirmation.
4. Report an OTP/boarding issue if verification fails.

## During the Trip

Use the app to:

- View route progress
- Track the cab
- Confirm safe reach where required
- Raise safety or lost-property incidents
- Create support tickets

## Safety

Use SOS for an immediate emergency or safety concern. Provide:

- Active route context
- GPS location
- Reason or details where available

For immediate physical danger, contact local emergency services.

## Trip Feedback

After completion, submit:

- Star rating
- Safety response
- Comment
- Feedback tags
