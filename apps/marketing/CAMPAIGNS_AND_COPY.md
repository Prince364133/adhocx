## 5. Tone of Voice & Writing Style Guidelines

1. **Professional & Operational:** Avoid hyperbole. Focus on concrete metrics, SLAs, and direct financial benefits.
2. **Clear & Action-Oriented:** CTAs must explicitly state the user's benefit (e.g., *"Claim ₹2,000 Bonus"*, *"Request Enterprise Fleet Access"*).
3. **Respectful to Drivers:** Speak to drivers as respected business partners, emphasizing fair pay, safety, dignity, and steady livelihoods.
4. **Authoritative on Safety & Compliance:** Reassure corporate clients with explicit details on background verification, GPS tracking, and insurance safeguards.
