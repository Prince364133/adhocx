1. Parties, Scope and Acceptance

1.1 These Terms govern access to the AdhocX website at www.adhocx.com, mobile applications and associated vendor, driver, vehicle-partner and authorised administration services (the Platform). AdhocX Technologies Private Limited is referred to as AdhocX, we, us or the Company. The initial service area is Hyderabad, Telangana, India; a booking must be within the service area and vehicle permissions shown for that booking.

1.2 A Vendor is a transport business or its authorised representative seeking commercial vehicles and drivers for commercial transport requirements. A Driver Partner is the approved individual who drives the assigned vehicle. A Vehicle Partner is the vehicle owner or authorised operator supplying that vehicle, whether driving personally or appointing an approved driver.

1.3 The registered Vendor and identified transport provider are responsible for the booking commitments they accept. AdhocX separately undertakes the platform, payment, refund and support obligations stated here.

1.4 Express acceptance must be recorded through an unticked acceptance control or an equivalent affirmative action with a version and timestamp. Merely viewing the marketing website is not acceptance of a paid booking or driver engagement.

2. Eligibility, Accounts and Verification

2.1 Account holders must be at least 18 years old and legally capable of contracting. Drivers must additionally satisfy every higher age, licence, badge, authorisation and fitness requirement applicable to their commercial vehicle and route.

2.2 Onboarding requires driving licence, lawful identity verification, PAN, vehicle registration, permits, fitness certificate, insurance, pollution-control documentation where required, and verified bank details.

2.3 Registration does not guarantee approval, assignments or minimum earnings. Verification is a check of the evidence available at that time. Where information cannot be verified, AdhocX may request clarification or restrict the affected activity and provide a review route.

3. Commercial Vehicles and Independent Partners

3.1 Only vehicles lawfully registered and permitted for commercial taxi/transport use may operate. They must display commercial number plates (yellow plates) and maintain valid permits, roadworthiness, fitness, and insurance. Privately registered vehicles are strictly ineligible.

3.2 No unapproved substitution of driver or vehicle is permitted. Any replacement must be recorded and communicated to the Vendor before the replacement service starts.

3.3 Partners participate as independent providers paid per trip under the agreed rate card, not as salaried AdhocX employees under these Terms.

3.4 Partners provide the lawful vehicle and driving service and are responsible for ordinary fuel, maintenance, repairs and business operating costs. Reimbursable tolls, parking, and waiting time must be specified or separately approved; there is no right to demand undisclosed extra cash from a Vendor or passenger.

4. Vendor Requirements and Automatic Assignment

4.1 The Vendor must supply accurate vehicle type, quantity, pickup/drop locations, scheduled date/time, and service instructions.

Automatic Assignment Rule: A driver's valid acceptance finalises assignment automatically when recorded by the Platform against available booking capacity. There is no second Vendor confirmation step. The Vendor is notified immediately, and the driver proceeds to pickup.

4.2 Payment and posting authorise AdhocX to seek eligible supply; search status, assigned quantities, and the 15-minute search cutoff are visible throughout the booking lifecycle.

5. Trip Performance and Safety

5.1 Approved drivers must attend the correct pickup, follow lawful instructions, use the agreed vehicle, and observe rest periods, seat-belt rules, and fatigue limits. Drivers must not drive while impaired or interact unsafely with the app.

5.2 Record arrival and trip progress accurately. Where a start OTP is required, obtain and validate it strictly through the authorized trip workflow. Do not spoof GPS, fabricate distance, or transfer identity.

5.3 In an emergency, first contact public emergency services (112/100) and take immediate safety precautions. WhatsApp support is an operational channel, not an emergency dispatch service.

6. Vendor Payments, Rates and Commission

6.1 Vendors pay AdhocX through disclosed checkout channels before booking activation. Checkout displays complete breakdown of service price, tax, and cancellation terms.

6.2 Driver earnings follow the accepted rate card for that specific offer. The Vendor price and driver rate may differ; AdhocX retains the agreed commercial commission/margin.

6.3 No arbitrary deductions, undisclosed penalties, or retroactive rate reductions may be imposed on earned partner amounts.

7. Driver Earnings Balance and Withdrawals

7.1 The earnings screen records amounts payable for completed eligible services, disclosed additions, and payouts.

1-Hour Daily Withdrawal Commitment: A Driver Partner may submit one withdrawal request per day (daily reset from 00:00 to 23:59 IST) for their available earnings balance. AdhocX guarantees requested funds reach the verified bank account within one hour of a valid request.

7.2 Account suspension or closure does not cause earned money to expire; undisputed payable balances remain legally due to the partner.

8. Search Cutoff, Cancellations and Refunds

15-Minute Search Cutoff Rule: The search cutoff is 15 minutes before the scheduled pickup time in Indian Standard Time (e.g. 8:45 PM cutoff for a 9:00 PM booking). This cutoff ends an unfulfilled supply search automatically.

8.1 If no driver is assigned at the 15-minute cutoff, AdhocX automatically cancels the unfulfilled booking and initiates a full refund.

1-Hour Vendor Refund Commitment: For unfulfilled bookings, an eligible refund reaches the Vendor's bank account within one hour of the cancellation trigger via instant banking rails.

8.2 For wholly unfulfilled trips, the full paid amount is refunded. No cancellation penalty or platform fee deduction applies to cancellation caused by lack of supply.

8.3 If an assigned driver cancels before the cutoff, AdhocX may continue searching for an eligible replacement until the cutoff. If a replacement is found, assignment finalizes automatically.

9. Acceptable Use and Platform Rights

9.1 Users must not misuse personal data, harass others, create fake trips, manipulate rates, evade safety checks, or engage in unlawful transport.

9.2 Contact information may be used only for active booking fulfillment, safety, or dispute resolution. Publishing partner documents or sending unsolicited contact after trip completion is prohibited.

10. Privacy and Communications

10.1 Personal data, document collection, bank details, and live GPS tracking are governed by the separate Privacy Policy.

10.2 Service messages regarding bookings, offers, payouts, and safety updates are delivered via app, SMS, email, or WhatsApp.

11. Suspension, Closure and Disruption

11.1 AdhocX may restrict account activity for expired document approvals, material breach, suspected fraud, safety issues, or legal requirements. Decisions are documented and subject to human review upon request.

11.2 Users may close accounts at any time after resolving pending trips and payouts.

12. Liability, Insurance and Recovery

12.1 Vehicle Partners must maintain valid commercial vehicle insurance covering driver and third-party liabilities as required under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.

12.2 Neither party is liable for remote indirect business losses. Nothing excludes statutory liability for personal injury, gross negligence, or mandatory consumer/data protection rights.

13. Complaints, Applicable Law and Jurisdiction

13.1 Indian law applies. Courts in Hyderabad, Telangana have jurisdiction over disputes arising under these Terms.

13.2 Complaints handling standard: Acknowledgement within 48 hours and resolution within one month.
