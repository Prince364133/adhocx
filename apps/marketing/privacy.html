1. Who We Are and When This Policy Applies

1.1 AdhocX Technologies Private Limited (AdhocX, we, us or the Company) determines the purposes and means of the processing described in this policy, subject to the roles applicable under Indian law. It applies to personal information handled through www.adhocx.com, AdhocX apps and related onboarding, operations and support channels.

1.2 It covers Vendor representatives, Driver Partners, Vehicle Partners, website interest registrants and people who contact us. A Vehicle Partner may drive their own vehicle or appoint an approved driver. Both the owner and actual driver have rights in their own information. A Vendor's or owner's acceptance does not replace any notice or consent that must be given to the individual concerned.

1.3 The initial operational market is Hyderabad, India. We apply the Indian privacy, information-security and sector rules applicable to the processing at the relevant time. The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) framework has phased commencement; this policy does not suggest every deferred provision is already enforceable. We will make the rights and safeguards required by each applicable phase available when required.

1.4 The Terms and Conditions explain service commitments and payments. This Privacy Policy is a notice about personal information, not blanket consent to all possible data uses. Where consent is required, it must be obtained for the specific purpose through the relevant notice or permission.

3. Identity Checks and Aadhaar Safeguards

3.1 Driving eligibility and vehicle permissions are distinct from identity verification. Provide authentic, current documents; we must explain the reason for a requested document. We may seek additional clarification only where necessary for that reason, a genuine mismatch or a legal requirement.

3.2 Aadhaar collection or verification must follow the law applicable to the chosen method. We will explain whether it is voluntary, the purpose, information used, verification recipient and available alternative. Aadhaar will not be made compulsory merely by inserting it into this policy.

3.3 Prefer an appropriate masked copy, permitted offline verification or another lawful method that minimises collection of the Aadhaar number. Do not upload unmasked Aadhaar to ordinary support chat. Core biometric information and Aadhaar authentication credentials are not requested under this policy. Do not send passwords or an authentication OTP to a support agent.

3.4 Access to identity documents must be restricted to authorised personnel or lawfully appointed verification services with a need to perform the check. Vendors and other Partners must not receive unrestricted access to another person's Aadhaar, PAN, bank account or full KYC files.

3.5 Retain a verification result or reduced record where it is sufficient and lawful, rather than keeping a complete document indefinitely.

4. Driver Location and Device Permissions

4.1 Precise location is used when the driver selects online availability and while the driver is proceeding to pickup or performing an active assignment/trip. This can include background collection when the app is not visible, if the app needs it for that disclosed service and the driver has granted device permission and consent.

4.2 We will show a clear location notice before requesting permission. Ordinary browsing of the public website is not permission for continuous driver tracking. The app will visibly indicate online/trip status and provide a practical way to go offline when no trip is active.

4.3 Continuous service tracking stops when the driver is offline and no assignment/trip remains active. Completing a trip while staying online still permits location use to find the next offer; going offline does not silently stop an active trip's disclosed tracking.

4.4 You can change location permission in device settings. Without the location needed for dispatch or active service, affected features may be unavailable and support may need to arrange a safe handover.

4.5 The assigned Vendor may see the relevant driver's trip location/status for fulfillment and monitoring. Full historical movement outside that Vendor's authorised service must not be shared with it. GPS records must not become an unrestricted record of private off-duty movement.

4.6 Camera or file access, if used for document submission, must be limited to the upload function and explicit user permission. This policy does not authorise access to contact books, call logs, microphones or unrelated photos.

5. Lawful Use, Choices and Communications

5.1 We process information for specific service purposes, legal obligations, and valid statutory grounds. Where consent is necessary, it must be informed, specific, and capable of withdrawal.

5.2 Account security, trip updates, and payment notifications are operational communications. Registering interest allows us to respond to launch inquiries. Promotional marketing requires separate opt-in consent and includes a workable opt-out mechanism.

5.3 We do not sell personal information or use submitted KYC and precise location for unrelated targeted advertising. We do not obtain permission to train unrelated general-purpose AI systems on driver, identity, bank, or location records.

5.4 Matching algorithms use availability, vehicle eligibility, service location, and assignment status. A user can ask support to review a material eligibility restriction, verification mismatch, payment hold, or matching error rather than receiving an unexplained automated refusal.

6. Who May Receive Information

6.1 Within an assigned service, Vendors and Driver/Vehicle Partners receive only operational contact details necessary to fulfill the booking. Initial offers do not reveal full identity files.

6.2 Authorised AdhocX operations, complaints, finance, and verification personnel access information strictly according to role and need.

6.3 Service providers process limited information for hosting, mapping (Google Maps), verification, messaging, payment collection, banking, or technical support under binding contractual safeguards.

6.4 Support messages sent to our WhatsApp number (+91 9390366116) are handled under WhatsApp's applicable privacy policy; sensitive identity uploads are directed to protected internal upload channels.

7. Payments and Account Credentials

7.1 Vendors pay AdhocX, and Partner earnings follow agreed rate cards paid to verified bank beneficiaries. Displayed earnings record money payable and do not constitute an interest-bearing bank account or regulated deposit wallet.

7.2 Only provide payment authentication through secure bank/payment provider interfaces. AdhocX support never asks for bank passwords, UPI PINs, card CVVs, or OTPs. Passwords are stored using strong salted hashes, never readable plaintext.

7.3 Payout/refund destination changes require proportionate identity checks to prevent money diversion.

8. Passenger and Third-Party Information

8.1 Current product scope does not deliberately collect passenger profiles, identity documents, or employee rosters. Vendors must not insert passenger personal data into free-text booking notes.

8.2 Pickup/drop addresses and route histories are treated as personal data where applicable and disclosed strictly for trip fulfillment, safety, or legal obligations.

9. Security, Safeguards and Data Transfers

9.1 We require technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to data risks: encrypted transmission (TLS 1.3), controlled administrative role access, logging, document access restrictions, backups, and vulnerability handling.

9.2 In the event of a security incident, we investigate, take containment steps, and notify affected users and competent authorities as required by Indian law within applicable limits.

10. Retention and Deletion Schedule

10.1 We retain information only for documented service purposes, dispute resolution, security, or legal accounting requirements.

10.2 Account and onboarding records are reviewed upon account closure. Raw location history has a limited retention period tied directly to dispatch, trip safety, and active dispute reviews. Financial transaction records are retained according to statutory accounting requirements.

10.3 When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or irreversibly anonymized.
