Why Legacy Corporate Transport Fails Enterprise Procurement

For decades, Indian corporate employee transportation has been held hostage by multi-layered vendor cartels, unverified subcontracting, and opaque trip billing. We re-engineered the entire operational model from first principles.

The Legacy Vendor Broker Model

  • • Multi-tiered Subcontracting: Primary vendors sublet demand to secondary and tertiary middlemen, diluting accountability and inflating costs by 20–35%.
  • • Falsified Logsheets & Ghost Kilometers: Manual trip sheets and disconnected GPS devices result in inaccurate billing and unverified employee drops.
  • • Severe Driver Attrition: Middlemen delay driver payouts by 45–90 days, creating sudden no-shows and shift disruptions for campus operations.
  • • Compliance Blindspots: Private vehicles (white plates) and expired fitness certificates slip past manual campus security gate checks.
The AdhocX Standard

AdhocX Direct-to-Driver Fleet Engine

  • • Direct Corporate Contract: Enterprise clients contract directly with AdhocX. No vendor intermediaries, broker commissions, or sub-leasing cartels.
  • • Automated 3-Way Match Billing: Every billable kilometer is verified against approved roster requests, actual PostGIS GPS breadcrumbs, and contracted rate cards.
  • • Direct Bank Payouts to Drivers: Independent cab-owning drivers receive automated weekly bank transfers with zero platform deduction on corporate routes.
  • • 100% Commercial RTA Verification: Every cab is independently verified for commercial yellow-board registration, active passenger insurance, and driver TR badges.
