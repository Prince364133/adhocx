Industry Terminology

Commercial Transport & ETS Glossary

Clear definitions of essential corporate mobility, fleet operations, and statutory transport concepts.

ETS (Employee Transport Software)

An automated digital platform that manages corporate employee shift scheduling, algorithm-based nodal routing, real-time driver allocation, live GPS trip telemetry, and end-of-month digital GST billing.

Nodal Pickup

A designated transit hub point where multiple employees residing in adjacent neighborhoods gather for pooled pickup, maximizing fleet vehicle occupancy (e.g. 7-seater Ertiga/Innova) and reducing transit duration.

Yellow-Board Commercial Cab

A passenger motor cab carrying registered yellow commercial license plates, commercial vehicle fitness certification, commercial third-party passenger insurance, and driven by a driver with an authorized commercial transport badge.

Escort Guard Protocol

Statutory safety requirement in Telangana and major IT corridors mandating a verified security escort inside the vehicle when female employees are picked up before 06:00 AM or dropped home after 08:00 PM.

Three-Way Invoice Match

An enterprise accounting audit control that matches the planned employee roster schedule against real-time geofenced GPS telemetry and the vendor line-item invoice before issuing financial payment.

Cutoff SLA

The strict operational threshold (15 minutes on AdhocX) within which the dispatch algorithm must confirm a verified commercial vehicle. If unfulfilled, funds automatically refund directly to the bank account within 1 hour.
