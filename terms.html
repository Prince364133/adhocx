2. Commercial Fleet & Driver Requirements

All Drivers on the AdhocX network explicitly agree to and warrant the following:

  • All vehicles operated must be registered with commercial yellow boards (commercial transport category).
  • Drivers must possess a valid Commercial Driving License with required state transport authority badges.
  • Vehicles must maintain up-to-date third-party and passenger commercial insurance, fitness certificates, and pollution control compliance.
  • Drivers agree to adhere to corporate safety standards, passenger dignity policies, and speed limit adherence.

3. 15-Minute Search Cutoff & 1-Hour Bank Refund SLA

When an Enterprise Booker initiates a ride or shift requirement on the AdhocX platform, our automated dispatch algorithm searches for the optimal verified commercial driver or fleet. If a ride cannot be confirmed within fifteen (15) minutes, the booking is automatically marked unfulfilled, and any prepaid booking fees or security holds are triggered for return directly to the corporate bank account within one (1) hour via instant banking rails.

Upon completing one hundred (100) verified, non-fraudulent trips within thirty (30) calendar days from account activation, a bonus of ₹2,000 (Two Thousand Indian Rupees) will be deposited into the driver's verified bank account in the subsequent payout cycle. AdhocX reserves the right to audit trips against GPS logs to prevent fraudulent trip completions.
