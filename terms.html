1. Commercial Marketplace Operating Model

AdhocX operates an automated, programmatic B2B dispatch and fulfillment marketplace connecting corporate enterprises ("Enterprise Bookers") with licensed commercial vehicle operators and independent chauffeurs ("Driver Partners") across Telangana (Hyderabad Tech Corridors & Regional Hubs). AdhocX enforces commercial motor vehicle compliance, transparent fixed tariffs, and programmatic dispatch telemetry.

2. Yellow-Board Commercial Fleet Requirements & Telangana Compliance

AdhocX exclusively permits commercial yellow-plate motor cabs in 4-seater (Sedan) and 6/7-seater (MUV/SUV) categories. Private white-board passenger vehicles are strictly prohibited from fulfillment under applicable state on-demand technology aggregator rules.

  • Drivers must possess a valid Commercial Driving License with required state transport authority badges (including Telangana RTO commercial badges and regional transport badges).
  • Vehicles must maintain up-to-date third-party and passenger commercial insurance, fitness certificates, and pollution control compliance.
  • Driver Partners agree to adhere to corporate safety standards, passenger dignity policies, and speed limit adherence.

3. 15-Minute Search Cutoff & 1-Hour Bank Refund SLA

When an Enterprise Booker initiates a ride or shift requirement on the AdhocX platform, our automated dispatch algorithm searches for the optimal verified fleet partner. If a ride cannot be confirmed within fifteen (15) minutes, the booking is automatically marked unfulfilled, and any prepaid booking fees or security holds are triggered for return directly to the corporate bank account within one (1) hour via instant banking rails.

4. Driver Partner Milestone Incentive Terms

Newly verified Driver Partners are eligible for the introductory Milestone promotional incentive program. Upon completing one hundred (100) verified, non-fraudulent trips within thirty (30) calendar days from account activation, the milestone incentive will be deposited directly into the driver's verified bank account in the subsequent payout cycle. AdhocX reserves the right to audit trips against GPS logs to prevent fraudulent trip completions.

5. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this platform shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.
