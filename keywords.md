# AdhocX Keyword Research

Prepared 2026-11-18 for https://www.adhocx.com (corporate employee transport / ETS, Hyderabad).

> **Read this first.** The research tools available here are plain web search and page fetching, not Ahrefs/Semrush/Google Keyword Planner. This file has **no search-volume or difficulty numbers**. Keywords come from competitor titles, descriptions, `keywords` meta tags and headings, plus how providers describe themselves in search results. Priorities are my judgement from intent and competition. Before spending on content, validate the Tier 1 list in Google Keyword Planner (free) or Search Console once the site has impressions.

**Observed positioning gaps you can use**
- Routematic and MoveInSync target large enterprises nationally. Few providers speak to **Hyderabad corridors** (HITEC City, Gachibowli, Financial District, Madhapur) with local content.
- Competitors' titles are generic ("Streamlined Employee Transport Management System"). TransRentals' meta keywords are the clearest local template: `corporate car rental Hyderabad, employee transport Hyderabad, business car rental Hyderabad, office cab Hyderabad, corporate taxi Hyderabad`.
- Safety and women-night-shift compliance is a strong buyer concern. Telangana's exemption requires written consent, free door-to-door transport, GPS tracking and security, and routing that avoids a woman being first pickup or last drop ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/employee-rights-labour-relations/845828/extension-of-365-day-operation-exemption-and-permission-for-women-working-at-night-in-telangana), [greytHR](https://www.greythr.com/notifications/telangana-exempts-establishments-to-employ-women-in-night-shifts/)). Few competitor pages target this.
- Routematic cites that 60% of GCCs lack integrated commute systems ([TimesTech](https://timestech.in/routematic-60-of-gccs-still-lack-integrated-commute-systems-amid-rising-esg-needs/)), and Hyderabad is GCC-heavy.

## 4. How to use these

1. **Titles and H1s**: put the Tier 1 keyword first, brand last, under 60 characters. Example for `/services`: `Employee Transport Service in Hyderabad | AdhocX`.
2. **One primary keyword per page.** Don't target "corporate car rental" and "employee transport software" on the same URL.
3. **Fill the gaps first:** women night-shift compliance page, HITEC City / Gachibowli local sections, and a competitor comparison page.
4. **Validate before writing:** paste Tier 1 and 2 into Google Keyword Planner for volume; add Search Console queries after 4–6 weeks.
5. **Avoid**: FAQ-schema-for-SERP strategies (retired May 2026) and mixing driver-hiring and buyer keywords on one page.

## 5. Not covered / caveats

- No volume, CPC or difficulty data (tools unavailable).
- Competitor pages for CabBazar, Nizam Cabs and BookMyCab were blocked by bot protection, so their keywords come from search snippets only.
- The Easycab I checked was a Jodhpur taxi service, unrelated to ETS. Remove it from the competitor list unless you meant another company.
- Competitor claims (fleet size, savings %) are self-reported marketing.
