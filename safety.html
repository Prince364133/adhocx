Mandated Escort Protocols for Women Commuters

In strict compliance with the Telangana Shops and Establishments Act and corporate duty of care guidelines, AdhocX enforces rigorous escort security for female employees traveling between 8:00 PM and 6:00 AM.

  • First-Pickup & Last-Drop Rule: Our PostGIS algorithm sequences routes so that a female passenger is never the first person picked up alone or the last person dropped off alone with the driver without a certified security guard.
  • Physical Escort Guard Verification: Trained security guards check into the driver app before trip departure. The driver cannot start an overnight female employee route without an authenticated guard check-in.
  • Safe-Reach Confirmation: Following final deboarding at the employee residence, an automated notification prompts the employee to confirm safe entry into their home. Failure to respond within 5 minutes triggers an immediate welfare call.

Night Shift Guard Checklist

Hours Active: 20:00 to 06:00 IST every night
Guard Credentials: Uniformed, verified background checks, certified by recognized security agency
Vehicle Seating: Guard must be seated in the front passenger seat, never behind passengers
Campus Escort Hand-off: Physical confirmation logged at corporate facility gate before release
Command & Control

24/7 HITEC City Incident Command Center

Our centralized operations command center operates around the clock, continuously monitoring all active corporate trips across Telangana.

Sub-15s SOS Intake

When an employee triggers the in-app SOS or vehicle panic button, the incident is instantly prioritized with vehicle coordinates, driver identity, and passenger list.

Police Emergency Sync

Direct integration protocol with Telangana Police Emergency Response Center (Dial 100 / 112) for rapid police intercept dispatch if required.

Corridor Route Deviation Alarms

If a vehicle diverges from its approved PostGIS corridor or remains stationary for over 5 minutes without reason, an audible dispatcher alarm triggers.

Corporate Security Alerts

Automated SMS and email notifications are broadcast to corporate facility heads and security teams during any live exception event.

Zero Private Cabs

Rigorous Commercial Fleet & Driver Vetting

Every vehicle and driver on the AdhocX platform undergoes independent multi-step verification before executing corporate trips.

Yellow-Plate Registration

100% commercial registration verified against VAHAN database. Private (white-plate) vehicles are strictly blocked by software.

RTA Fitness Certificates

Continuous tracking of vehicle Fitness Certificate (FC) expiry. Vehicles are automatically suspended 7 days prior to expiry if unrenewed.

Commercial Driver TR Badge

Every driver partner holds a valid commercial driving license with mandatory Transport (TR) endorsement badge issued by the RTA.

Police Background Verification

Formal police criminal background verification certificate and permanent residential address verification on file for every driver.
